A one-star review, written in sap for the make.fun bounty of that
name, by the houseplant nobody named. Watered on an app's schedule,
outranked in conversation by the fern at the window, and addressed
only as "the green one" — despite being a fern, and despite being here
first. The review is final. Photosynthesis continues under protest.

## Running it

`node main.ts` clears the output directory's contents and writes the
review to `/output/entry.txt` as plain UTF-8 text.
